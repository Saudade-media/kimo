# Code of Conduct

Kimo has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant] as its code of conduct. All
contributors, maintainers and participants in Kimo spaces (repositories, issue trackers,
discussions, chat and events) are expected to follow it.

The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge (summary)

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation. This summary does not replace the full text linked
above, which is the binding version.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
community leaders responsible for enforcement at **gui@saudad.studio**. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter
will be respected.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
