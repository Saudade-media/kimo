# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as the decision taken

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or roles
- **Teardown refs:** sections of [docs/teardown.md](../teardown.md) this decision implements or changes

## Context

What problem forces a decision now? State the constraints, the forces in tension and the facts
(with sources). Keep it neutral: a reader should be able to disagree with the decision after
reading only this section.

## Decision

The decision, in one or two sentences first, then the details that make it concrete (interfaces,
names, rules, numbers). Use "must" / "must not" for anything other code or docs rely on.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to build, test or document, and
which follow-up ADRs or milestones this triggers. Include negative consequences.

## Alternatives considered

For each serious alternative: what it is, and why it was not chosen. "Do nothing" counts when it
was a real option.

## Revisit when

Optional. The concrete signal (milestone, measurement, upstream release) that should reopen this
decision.
