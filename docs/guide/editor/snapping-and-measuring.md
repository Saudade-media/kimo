# Snapping and measuring

## Snapping

When you move or resize a layer, its edges and center snap to its siblings and its parent, and to equal spacing between siblings. Snap markers show what it lined up with. Snapping kicks in within 5 screen pixels.

- Hold `⌘` while dragging to turn snapping off for that drag.
- Snapping is also off while you resize with `⇧` (keep proportions) or `⌥` (from the center).

## Measuring

Select a layer, keep the Move tool, and hold `⌥`. Hover another layer to see the distances between the two; hover elsewhere to see the distances to the parent's edges. A **Measuring** pill shows while it is active.

## Pixel grid

Above 400 % zoom, a pixel grid fades in. While it shows, moves and resizes land on whole pixels, the zoom badge appears, and a readout follows the pointer with its position and the color under it (`x 22 · y 14 · E76F51 66%`).

> [!NOTE]
> Snapping and the pixel grid are always on; there is no setting to turn them off yet. Hold `⌘` to drag without snapping.
